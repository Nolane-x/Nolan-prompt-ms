## Primitive Competition Protocol

Do not run primitive competition before proving there is behavior worth preserving.

### Phase P0 — Utility gate

Compare matched fresh tasks:

- `U0`: no added guidance;
- `U1`: current 446-word Verified Delta force-loaded.

If `U1` does not show a reproducible practically meaningful benefit without unacceptable regressions/cost, stop. Do not optimize metadata, rivals, or wording to rescue the project.

### Phase P1 — Frozen rival competition

Only after U1 earns utility, preregister and freeze materially different candidates before selection-validation:

`R1 full incumbent | R2 compressed incumbent | R3 evidence-gated loop | R4 goal/invariant contract | R5 verification kernel | R0 no guidance`

Evaluate on identical task pairs and retain component outcomes rather than one scalar. At minimum record:

- target-state success;
- serious/catastrophic failure count;
- invariant violations;
- false completion;
- unnecessary actions and overthinking;
- scope expansion/abstention errors;
- token/context cost;
- tool calls and latency/resource cost when measurable;
- per-task stochastic reliability.

When candidate lengths differ materially, include a token-matched control where practical. A longer prompt does not receive credit merely for supplying more text.

### Phase P2 — Decision law

A materially shorter candidate defeats the incumbent when it is non-inferior on preregistered success and serious-failure criteria while being strictly cheaper on the relevant deployment cost. A rival that wins only on development probes but loses fresh selection-validation does not replace the incumbent.

If two candidates occupy different Pareto frontiers, retain the tradeoff until the intended workload supplies weights. Do not collapse conflicting dimensions into a convenient score after seeing results.

Only after primitive selection may semantic ablation optimize the winning family. Ablating the incumbent before challenging its abstraction risks perfecting the wrong representation.

- agent-evaluation methodology requires isolated trials, explicit graders, task-level pairing, outcome checks, transcript inspection, and fresh validation after adaptive selection.
